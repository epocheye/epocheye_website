laterite_grain_512.png is a greyscale grain tile made from Poly Haven's "Red Laterite Soil Stones"
(https://polyhaven.com/a/red_laterite_soil_stones), diffuse map at 2k, as copied into the palace
assets at E:/heritage_assets/tipu-summer-palace-bengaluru/output/mobile/tex/003_red_laterite_soil_stones_diff_2k_jpg.jpg.
Licence: CC0 1.0 (https://polyhaven.com/license).
Made by dividing the luminance by its own 48 px wrap-around blur (so only the grain is left and the
tile still repeats cleanly), clipping at two standard deviations and resizing to 512 px.
The game multiplies the baked ground by it near the player, so it carries no colour.
