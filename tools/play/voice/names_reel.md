## Notes per name

- **Hyder Ali**: All three engines produce HY-der by default. Kokoro American stresses the second syllable of Ali (ah-LEE), Kokoro British and MeloTTS the first (AH-lee). Left as the engines say it.
- **Tipu Sultan**: Engines say TIP-oo by default (short i). Overridden to TEE-poo. Sultan is left as the English dictionary word (SUL-tun). In India it is usually said sool-TAHN. Owner to choose: to switch, add Sultan with kokoro_a 'sʊltˈɑn', kokoro_b 'sʊltˈɑːn', arpabet 'S UH0 L T AA1 N', espeak 'sʊltˈɑːn'.
- **Srirangapatna**: Engine defaults were wrong (for example Kokoro: SRUR-ung-gap-at-nuh). Overridden.
- **Bangalore**: Engine defaults are the English dictionary form, with the main stress on the last syllable in Kokoro (bang-guh-LOR).
- **Kempe Gowda**: Engines dropped the final e of Kempe (KEMP). Overridden to KEM-pay. Gowda is GOW-duh by default in all three.
- **Chikka Devaraja Wadiyar**: Devaraja: defaults were di-vuh-RAH-juh (Kokoro, Piper) and di-var-JEE-uh (MeloTTS). Overridden to DAY-vuh-RAH-juh. Wadiyar: MeloTTS guessed wah-DIV-er, overridden. Kokoro and Piper say WAH-di-yar (WOD-i-yar in British voices) by default.
- **Kote Venkataramana**: Engines said KOHT (one syllable) and ven-kuh-tar-uh-MAH-nuh or ven-kuh-tar-AY-muh-nuh. Overridden.
- **Attara Kacheri**: Kacheri: engines said KASH-er-ee or kuh-SHER-ee. Overridden to kuh-CHAY-ree. Attara: Kokoro doubled the r, overridden; MeloTTS and Piper say uh-TAH-ruh by default.
- **Mysore**: MeloTTS guessed mis-OR, overridden. Kokoro says my-SOR by default. Piper says MY-sor (first syllable stressed).
- **Buchanan**: Engine defaults (byoo-KAN-un). Francis Buchanan was Scottish; buh-KAN-un is also heard.
- **Cornwallis**: Kokoro and Piper ended it in -eez with the stress on the first syllable. Overridden. MeloTTS has it in its dictionary (korn-WAW-lus).
- **Bowring**: No primary source for the pronunciation was found on the day (Wikipedia's article on John Bowring gives none). Left as the engines say it, BOH-ring. If the owner knows it as BOW-ring: kokoro_a 'bˈWɹɪŋ', kokoro_b 'bˈWɹɪŋ', arpabet 'B AW1 R IH0 NG', espeak 'bˈaʊɹɪŋ'.
- **Hunter**: Ordinary English word. Engine defaults.
