The inscription, on a stone set into the wall. It opens with the laying of the foundation.

THE INSCRIPTION
“As soon as the foundation of this palace was laid, its head was raised to heaven with joy.”

A Persian verse on a stone set into the wall. Its two dates are hidden in two phrases: a house of happiness, and envied by heaven. The first is worked out in a letter and number system Tipu Sultan devised himself.

Translation: B. L. Rice, Epigraphia Carnatica IX, Bangalore no. 7. Public domain.

TIPU SULTAN'S SUMMER PALACE · BENGALURU
What you now know
1
By its own inscription: begun in 1781, in Hyder Ali's time; finished in 1791, under Tipu Sultan. The board at the entrance says the 1770s.
2
It stands inside what was Bangalore Fort. A gateway and a short run of wall are what survive.
3
Stone at the base, mud in the walls, teak for nearly everything you see.
4
Its own inscription calls it the Abode of Happiness and the Envy of Heaven.
5
The fort fell on 21 March 1791, the year the palace was completed.
6
The oldest dated pictures of it were drawn in February 1792 by James Hunter, a soldier in the army that took the fort. He died that year. They were printed in 1804.
7
The court ran on height: the ruler on an upper balcony, officers by rank, the public in the open court by the fountains.
8
The gold was false gilding. The private rooms were small.
9
The best description of the palace was written in 1800, a year after its court ended.
10
From 1831 to April 1868 it was the government's offices. They left partly from fear it would collapse.
11
Most of the palace was pulled down. What stands is one part of it.
12
Nearly all its painted decoration is gone, and nobody recorded when.
YOUR CHOICES
You first trusted the guidebooks over the surveyor.
You first chose to copy the colours of the prints exactly.
You first took the shine for gold leaf.
You chose to continue the surviving pattern, marked as our reconstruction.
Which year would you put back? 1800. As Buchanan found it, with the court gone.
WHAT NOBODY KNOWS
When the paint was lost. It was there in May 1800. There was whitewash by 1873.
Whether the palace was finished before or after the fort fell in 1791.
What the building was used for between 1800 and 1831.
The colours of anything Hunter did not draw and the building did not keep.
